# Foundation Metrics

## Step Conversion Rate
`Next Step Users ÷ Current Step Users × 100%`

## Drop-off Rate
`(Current Step Users - Next Step Users) ÷ Current Step Users × 100%`

## Activation Rate
`Activated New Users ÷ Eligible New Users × 100%`

## D7 Retention
`Day-7 Active Users from Cohort ÷ Cohort Size × 100%`

## Payer Conversion
`Paying Users ÷ Eligible Users × 100%`

## CAC
`Acquisition Cost ÷ New Customers`

## ARPU
`Revenue ÷ Defined User Base`

## ARPPU
`Revenue ÷ Paying Users`

## Simplified Revenue Tree
`Revenue = Active Users × Payer Conversion × ARPPU`

## Referral K
`K ≈ Average Invites per User × Invite Conversion`

## Relative Change
`(New - Old) ÷ Old × 100%`

## Percentage Point
`New Rate - Old Rate`

Always state metric definition and time window.
