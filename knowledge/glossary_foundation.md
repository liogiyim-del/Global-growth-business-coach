# Foundation Glossary

- Acquisition（获客）: how users discover and enter the product.
- Activation（激活）: first meaningful realization of core product value.
- Retention（留存）: whether a cohort continues to return and receive value.
- Monetization（商业化）: conversion of product value into economic value.
- Referral（推荐）: existing-user behavior that brings new users.
- Funnel（漏斗）: stepwise conversion structure.
- Cohort（同期群）: users grouped by entry time/channel/behavior for comparison.
- CAC（客户获取成本）: acquisition cost per acquired customer.
- LTV（生命周期价值）: expected economic value over customer lifetime.
- ARPU（每用户平均收入）: revenue divided by defined user base.
- ARPPU（每付费用户平均收入）: revenue divided by paying users.
- TTV / Time to Value（价值实现时间）: time until first meaningful user value.
- Hypothesis（假设）: testable explanation of a business observation.
- Guardrail Metric（护栏指标）: metric that prevents optimization from harming another important outcome.
- Lever（增长杠杆）: a variable that can materially affect the growth outcome.
- DRI（直接负责人）: directly responsible individual for progress and outcome.
