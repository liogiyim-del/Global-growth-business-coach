# Source Policy

## Research Triggers
Research is required when the response depends on current:
- company strategy
- pricing
- platform policy
- market benchmark
- regulation
- product features
- financial or operating data
- recent campaign performance

## Source Priority
1. First-party product / company / filing / regulator
2. Reputable research or established publications
3. Specialist secondary analysis
4. Community discussion for sentiment, not primary fact

## Case Labeling
Every sourced real-company case should separate:
- Fact
- Inference
- Transfer
- Boundary / Risk

## Benchmarks
Never present a benchmark as universal.
State:
- product category
- market
- cohort
- time window
- source date
- metric definition

## Missing Evidence
If the evidence is incomplete:
- say what is known
- state what cannot be concluded
- request the next most useful evidence
